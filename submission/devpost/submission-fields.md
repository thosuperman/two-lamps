## Testing instructions for judges (paste into the description or the testing field)

1. Open https://www.reddit.com/r/TwoLampsGame/ and find today's **Two Lamps #N** post. A new one appears every day at 00:00 UTC.
2. The post card shows today's puzzle. Tap **Play**.
3. Tap a tile to stack a block; long-press (or use **Remove**) to take one off. Fill both dashed shadows on the walls exactly. The lights above each column turn gold when that column matches.
4. When both shadows match, the windows light up and par is revealed. Keep building to reach par, or open **Results** to see the subreddit's histogram, post your score to the comments, or turn on streak flair.
5. Best experienced on desktop or in the Reddit app. On a phone's mobile browser, Reddit may show a "view in app" notice for new communities.
