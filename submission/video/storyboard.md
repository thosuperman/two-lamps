# Demo video storyboard

**Final cut:** `demo.mp4`, 57.0 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps, H.264 + AAC.

**Footage.** All gameplay comes from **one continuous screen recording** of the live app on **r/TwoLampsGame**, made in the iPhone 17 simulator (iOS 26.5) on Reddit mobile web and logged in as the developer.
- Ranges marked *sped up* are faster than real time, and the video labels them on screen.
- The weekday-twist stills are real screenshots of the posts for puzzles #2 and #3.

**Layout.** The phone sits on the left and captions on the right, over a dusk background drawn in code.

## Honesty notes

- Nothing is mocked. Every number on screen came from the live server.
- Sped-up footage is labelled **SPED UP**.
- "1 played today" and "You are the first to light the lamps" are true: r/TwoLampsGame was created that day.
- Weekday twists not shown in the video (Big Plot, Third Lamp, Grand Plot) are described only in the text.
